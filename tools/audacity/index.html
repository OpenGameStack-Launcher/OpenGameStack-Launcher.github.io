Audacity

Frozen in Launcher GPL-3.0

Fast, multitrack audio editor and recording tool for dialogue, foley, and sound effects.

Official Website Source Repository

Role in the Game Stack

Audacity is an accessible multitrack audio editor for recording live dialogue, trimming and normalizing sound effects, removing background noise, and exporting game-ready audio assets in OGG, WAV, and MP3 formats.

Key Capabilities & Strengths

  • Rapid multitrack waveform editing and clip trimming
  • Spectral frequency analysis and surgical noise reduction
  • Loudness normalization (LUFS) for broadcast and game audio standards
  • Native VST3, LV2, and LADSPA plugin support
Replaces Proprietary Software

Within sovereign and telemetry-free creative pipelines, Audacity serves as a direct open-source alternative to proprietary software.

Replaces: Adobe Audition Sound Forge
