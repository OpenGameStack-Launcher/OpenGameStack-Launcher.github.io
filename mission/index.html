Mission

The Declaration of Digital Sovereignty

We believe that the tools used to build the future should not be rented. They should be owned.

For too long, the pipeline of digital creation has been fragile. Studios rent their engines under shifting licensing terms. Developers fear the next mandatory update that breaks existing projects. Creators rely on black box proprietary software loaded with runtime telemetry and vendor kill-switches.

We propose a new standard: an open development environment where every creative tool is as permanent, auditable, and version-controlled as the project files themselves.

I. Sovereignty Over Subscription

Creativity requires enduring stability. When you build an interactive world, you must own the tools that sustain it. We reject subscription models where access to your own creative work depends on recurring monthly fees, cloud seat licenses, or remote server authentication. Open Game Stack is built exclusively on vetted open-source software that operates completely offline, without telemetry, and without requiring third-party permission.

II. The Environment is the Artifact

Source code without the correct compiler cannot build. A game asset without the exact matching version of Blender or Krita can suffer corrupted exports or broken material pipelines. To truly preserve a project, we must version-control the entire studio. When you clone an Open Game Stack project, you do not just pull the code; you obtain the exact, frozen tools required to build, edit, and export it.

III. Dual-Use by Design

What empowers the solo indie game developer also anchors the professional studio, educator, and simulation engineer. Indie creators need zero recurring costs, zero telemetry, and maximum creative freedom. Studios and classrooms need cross-team consistency, instant onboarding, and deterministic pipelines that can be safely archived for decades. Dual-use means engineering tools to meet rigorous standards for reliability, security, and long-term reproducibility while keeping them freely accessible to every creator. When tools serve both bedroom developers and production studios, digital sovereignty becomes universal.

IV. Stability is a Feature

Innovation is counterproductive if it constantly breaks production pipelines. We prioritize Long-Term Support (LTS) baselines and predictable manifest declarations over chasing unvetted features. By delivering verified, frozen tool versions through canonical manifests like ogs_stack.json, teams can scale smoothly without fear of dependency drift or sudden breaking changes.

This is our commitment: to build a foundation that does not move beneath your feet.
